Concept

This experience will heighten awareness of overfishing and illegal fishing by providing facts on the topic as well as interactive statistics and information about some affected species.

This will be portrayed by an interactive webpage, the movement down the page that is made, as well as the interactivity of illustrations and sub-pages for more in-depth details.

Overview

Overfishing is the concept of catching too many fish at one time, faster than nature allows them to reproduce.

This is mainly impacted by large contractor fishing vehicles, who often catch fish they don’t need, which can lead to the endangerment and extinction of certain species just by proxy. However, this issue is also heightened by illegal fishers, and the unfiltered grey market they are sold into.
